Sandra & Mathias: one wedding, three celebrations

We followed Sandra and Mathias from the civil ceremony through the dote to the church and reception, in photos and video, so the whole story of their wedding was told in one place.

Client
Sandra & Mathias
When
September 2026
Services
Wedding photography, video, print selection

The brief

Cover every part of a multi-day Rwandan wedding: the civil ceremony, the traditional dote and the church wedding with its reception, with a consistent look from start to finish.

What we did

One team covered all three days. We photographed the ceremonies, the families and the details, filmed the key moments, then edited a full gallery and a selection prepared for print.

What they got

  • An edited photo gallery of all three celebrations
  • A print-ready selection for albums and frames
  • Video coverage of the ceremonies and reception
